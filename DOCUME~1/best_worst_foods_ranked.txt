SOURCE: UTSA Reddit thread — "Rank every food on campus, I'll start" (500+ comments, Fall 2023)
Compiled by: moderator_utsa_dining

---

MOST UPVOTED "BEST" ITEMS ON CAMPUS:

1. Rowdy Cart street tacos (consensus #1 best value on campus)
"The al pastor tacos from the Rowdy Cart are genuinely restaurant quality. $3 each, fresh tortillas, they put the salsa on the side. I look forward to taco days more than anything else campus-related." — taco_devotee_utsa

"The Rowdy Cart has rotating specials and I've never had a bad one. The elote is incredible — grilled corn with cotija and chili lime. Gets me every time it's on the menu." — elote_stan_utsa

2. Roadrunner Café stir-fry station
"This is the sleeper hit of Roadrunner Café. You tell them your protein, vegetables, and sauce and they make it fresh in a hot wok right in front of you. Takes 4 minutes. The teriyaki chicken with broccoli is what I get 3x a week." — stir_fry_loyalist

"The stir-fry is 100% the best station at the dining hall. Everything else has been sitting under a heat lamp but this is genuinely fresh." — dining_hall_strategist

3. Roadrunner Café breakfast omelets
"The made-to-order omelets in the morning are legitimately good. Spinach, mushrooms, pepper jack. Hits different at 8am. You have to go before 10:30 though." — early_bird_utsa

4. Library Café Starbucks
"Full espresso drinks, reliable quality, the baristas actually know what they're doing. The only real coffee on campus." — coffee_dependent_student

5. Chick-fil-A (Sombrilla) — consensus quality pick, not value pick
"It's just good Chick-fil-A. You know what you're getting. The price is fine. The line is not fine." — chick_fil_a_appreciator

---

MOST UPVOTED "WORST" ITEMS ON CAMPUS:

1. Roadrunner Café pizza
"The pizza is a crime. Thin, soggy, barely any toppings, has clearly been sitting for 45 minutes by the time you get there. It's always there because nobody else wants it either." — pizza_hater_utsa

"The pizza at Roadrunner represents everything wrong with institutional food. Just avoid it." — strong_opinions_on_pizza

"Correction: the pizza is fine if you catch it right when they put it out which happens about every 40 minutes. Right out of the oven it's acceptable. Otherwise yes it's bad." — nuanced_pizza_take

2. Roadrunner Café weekend dinner
"Saturday night dinner at Roadrunner is depressing. Like 4 things are open, the food looks exhausted, and the dining hall itself is half empty and somehow feels lonelier for it." — weekend_dining_survivor

"Sunday night dinner is the only meal where I've genuinely considered not eating. The options are minimal and the quality matches." — extreme_statement_maybe_accurate

3. Jamba Juice smoothies (value complaint, not quality)
"The smoothies are actually fine but $9 for a medium is insane when I'm a broke college student. I use leftover Dining Dollars on these in December so technically free." — pragmatic_jamba_customer

4. Vending machine food (late night option)
"After 9pm on a Tuesday when you're studying for an exam, the choice between a $2.50 bag of Doritos and nothing is genuinely demoralizing. UTSA needs late night food options desperately." — night_owl_student

UNDERRATED OPTIONS:

"The Rowdy Cart gets mentioned a lot but the Korean BBQ food truck on Thursdays near the Engineering building is so slept on. Bibimbap for $9 that's actually good." — food_truck_explorer

"Which Wich in the Sombrilla. Always shorter line than Chick-fil-A, better quality sandwich, same price range. Nobody talks about it." — which_wich_advocate

"The grab-and-go section at the Library Café. If you need something fast between classes the pre-made wraps are decent and there's never a line." — efficiency_seeker_utsa

"The salad bar at Roadrunner Café is actually really good if you build it right. Load up on chickpeas, roasted beets, hard boiled eggs, and get the balsamic. Better than most of the hot food." — salad_bar_evangelist
