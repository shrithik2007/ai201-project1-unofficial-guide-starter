TIMING STRATEGIES

The single most impactful change you can make to your dining experience: stop eating at peak hours. Peak hours are 11:45am–1:15pm and 5:30pm–7:00pm. If your schedule allows any flexibility, eating at 11am or 1:30pm for lunch saves you 15-20 minutes of waiting and usually means fresher food from recently restocked stations.

The stir-fry station at Roadrunner Café is worth the 4-minute wait. Everything else that involves a line during peak hours is usually not. The pre-made hot food on steam tables doesn't get better from waiting.

At the Sombrilla, Chick-fil-A has the longest line by far. If you have 15 free minutes it's fine. If you're between classes and have 10 minutes, go to Which Wich or Panda Express instead — you'll actually make it back in time.

---

MONEY-SAVING STRATEGIES

Rowdy Bucks never expire. Dining Dollars expire at semester end. When you have both, spend Dining Dollars first. Load any extra money you add to your 1501 card as Rowdy Bucks, not Dining Dollars, unless you're certain you'll spend it this semester.

The Rowdy Cart is the best value on campus. Tacos are $3, a full meal is $8-10, and the quality beats the Sombrilla chains. Find the current location on Instagram @UTSADining before you make the walk.

The food trucks near Engineering (Tuesdays and Thursdays) are not on the meal plan but are competitively priced and high quality. Korean BBQ bibimbap for $9 is comparable to what you'd pay at a restaurant off campus.

If you have Dining Dollars to burn at the end of the semester, Jamba Juice is a good option — it's expensive normally but feels free when you're burning dollars before they expire.

FOOD QUALITY STRATEGIES

At Roadrunner Café, the made-to-order stations (stir-fry, grill, omelets) consistently beat the steam table food. The steam table food has been sitting; the made-to-order food hasn't. This is the #1 dining hall hack.

The pizza is only good for about 15 minutes after it comes out of the oven. If you want pizza, watch the station and time your approach. Asking staff "when does the next pizza come out?" is not a weird question — they'll tell you.

The salad bar is restocked multiple times a day and is almost always fresh. If you're skeptical of the hot food quality, a well-built salad with protein from the bar (hard-boiled eggs, chickpeas, edamame when available) is a reliable fallback.

Breakfast is the best meal at Roadrunner Café. The made-to-order omelets, fresh fruit, and waffle station are all reliably good. The lunch and dinner quality is more variable. If you can shift any meals to breakfast time you probably should.

PRACTICAL LOGISTICS

The Library Café Starbucks gets very crowded during midterms and finals — waits up to 20 minutes. Use the Starbucks app to pre-order your drink and pick it up at the mobile order counter instead of waiting in the regular line.

Roadrunner Café closes at 9pm Sunday–Thursday. If you're a night owl who studies late, make sure you eat before you settle in for the night — there's no food on campus after 9pm except vending machines.

Guest swipes: You have guest swipes on your meal plan (usually 5 per semester). These let you bring a friend into Roadrunner Café. This is great for study dates. Swipe at the entrance kiosk — it's easy to miss that you have this option.

For large study group snacks: The Sombrilla options are okay for individuals but awkward for group sharing. The H-E-B on Babcock Road (10 minute drive or rideshare) has much better value for snacks, drinks, and group food. HEB's prepared foods section has cheaper options than any campus dining location.

---

FOR ATHLETES AND HIGH-CALORIE NEEDS

The grill station at Roadrunner Café will give you larger portions if you ask. Staff are generally accommodating for students who need more food. The stir-fry station is also customizable — you can ask for extra protein.

The smoothie bar near the Roadrunner Café entrance is convenient but overpriced. The protein you need is available cheaper at the grill and salad bar. Use the smoothie bar only if convenience outweighs cost for you.

---

HIDDEN GEM: THE QUIET SEATING AREA

There's a smaller seating section at the back of Roadrunner Café near the vegan station that most students don't know about. During peak hours when the main area is noisy and every table is taken, this area often has open seats. It's also quieter, which matters if you're eating with reading or studying.
