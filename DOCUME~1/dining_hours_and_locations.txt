SOURCE: UTSA Student Forum — "Complete guide to campus dining locations and hours" (crowdsourced, updated Fall 2023)

This guide was put together by upperclassmen and updated each semester. Always verify hours on the official UTSA dining website since they change for finals, breaks, and special events.

---

ROADRUNNER CAFÉ (Main Campus, Rec Center area)
Type: All-you-can-eat swipe dining hall
Hours (Fall/Spring):
  Monday–Thursday: 7:00am – 9:00pm
  Friday: 7:00am – 8:00pm
  Saturday: 9:00am – 8:00pm (Brunch until 2pm, limited dinner after)
  Sunday: 9:00am – 8:00pm (Brunch until 2pm, limited dinner after)
Payment: Meal swipes, Dining Dollars, Rowdy Bucks, credit/debit
Notes: Busiest 11:45am–1:15pm and 5:30pm–7pm. Made-to-order stations close 30 min before closing.

---

THE SOMBRILLA (Main Campus, Business Building area)
Type: Food court / retail dining
Hours:
  Monday–Friday: 7:30am – 8:00pm
  Saturday: 10:00am – 4:00pm
  Sunday: Closed
Vendors: Chick-fil-A, Panda Express, Which Wich, Jamba Juice, Einstein Bros Bagels
Payment: Dining Dollars, Rowdy Bucks, credit/debit (meal swipes NOT accepted)
Notes: Chick-fil-A line is the longest — 15–25 min during lunch. Panda Express runs out of orange chicken by 1pm on busy days. Einstein Bros is best before 10am when bagels are fresh.

---

THE LIBRARY CAFÉ (John Peace Library, 1st floor)
Type: Grab-and-go café
Hours:
  Monday–Thursday: 7:30am – 10:00pm
  Friday: 7:30am – 6:00pm
  Saturday–Sunday: Closed
Offerings: Sandwiches, wraps, salads, pastries, Starbucks beverages
Payment: Dining Dollars, Rowdy Bucks, credit/debit
Notes: The Starbucks here has full espresso drinks. Gets very crowded during midterms and finals — waits can hit 20 minutes for espresso. Pre-order on the Starbucks app to skip the line. Grab-and-go section near the entrance is fast and underused.

---

JPL STARBUCKS (Jefferson Hall / JPL building)
Type: Licensed Starbucks
Hours:
  Monday–Friday: 7:00am – 7:00pm
  Saturday: 9:00am – 3:00pm
  Sunday: Closed
Payment: Starbucks app, Dining Dollars, Rowdy Bucks, credit/debit
Notes: Shorter lines than the Library Café location most mornings. Closes earlier. Popular with grad students and faculty.

---

THE ROWDY CART (various outdoor locations)
Type: Mobile food cart — location rotates
Common locations: Near the Convocation Center, outside the Science building, east entrance of the Rec Center
Hours: Varies — typically 10:30am – 2:30pm Monday–Friday
Offerings: Street tacos, tortas, elotes, agua frescas
Payment: Cash, Rowdy Bucks, credit/debit
Notes: This is a student favorite and consistently rated the best value on campus. Lines are shorter than anything in the Sombrilla. Follow @UTSADining on Instagram for daily location updates. Does not operate on rainy days.

---

CHICK-FIL-A (Sombrilla location)
Type: Fast food chain inside food court
Hours: Monday–Saturday 7:30am – 8:00pm (closed Sunday per brand policy)
Payment: Dining Dollars, Rowdy Bucks, credit/debit
Wait times: 5–10 min before 11am; 15–25 min 11am–2pm; 5–10 min after 2pm
Notes: If you want Chick-fil-A during lunch rush, arrive before 11:15am or after 1:30pm. The mobile order option through the Chick-fil-A app does NOT work at campus locations — you have to order in person.

---

FOOD TRUCKS (East side of campus, near Engineering building parking lot)
Type: Rotating food trucks — not UTSA-operated
Typical schedule: Tuesday and Thursday, 11am–2pm
Common trucks: Taco Loco, Curry Up Now (occasional), Korean BBQ on wheels
Payment: Cash or card depending on truck
Notes: These are independent operators, not affiliated with UTSA Dining. Not included in any meal plan. Best option for variety and value. Check the UTSA Events calendar or ask in r/UTSA for the current rotation.

---

LATE NIGHT OPTIONS (after 9pm):
- Vending machines: All academic buildings, 24/7
- The Sombrilla closes at 8pm; no on-campus food available after 9pm
- Nearest off-campus options: H-E-B on Babcock Rd (10 min drive), McDonald's on Fredericksburg Rd (7 min drive), multiple Taco Cabana locations
- Delivery services: DoorDash and Uber Eats both deliver to campus; use the "Meet at entrance" option since drivers can't always navigate campus roads at night
