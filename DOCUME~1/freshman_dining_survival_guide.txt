WEEK 1: WHAT TO EXPECT

Your first week of eating in Roadrunner Café will feel exciting — the variety seems overwhelming and you'll want to try everything. By week three it'll feel repetitive. This is normal. Here's how to manage it for a full semester.

The dining hall layout: When you first walk into Roadrunner Café, you swipe your card at the entrance kiosk. Meal swipes cost one swipe per entry; you'll see your remaining balance on the screen. The main floor has multiple stations arranged in stations around the edges and in islands in the center. Check the daily menu board as you walk in — it tells you what's available today, including which stations have specials.

Made-to-order vs. steam table: The steam table is the long counter with metal bins of food that's been cooking since it was placed there. Quality varies. The made-to-order stations are where staff cook or assemble food fresh when you ask. The stir-fry station, grill station, and breakfast omelet station are made-to-order. These are consistently better. Always prioritize them when the wait is under 5 minutes.

---

FIRST TWO WEEKS: GETTING ORIENTED

Find your preferred off-peak times: The dining hall is most crowded 11:45am–1:15pm and 5:30pm–7pm. If your schedule allows even a 30-minute shift, it makes a real difference. Try eating lunch at 11am or 1:30pm and see if that works with your class schedule.

Explore the Sombrilla: The food court near the Business building has Chick-fil-A, Panda Express, Which Wich, Einstein Bros, and Jamba Juice. Meal swipes are NOT accepted here — only Dining Dollars, Rowdy Bucks, or credit/debit. Good for variety but more expensive than the dining hall per effective cost.

Find the Rowdy Cart: Check @UTSADining on Instagram for the daily location of the Rowdy Cart. It's the best food deal on campus and a lot of freshmen don't find it until someone tells them about it.

---

MANAGING YOUR MEAL PLAN

Check your balance weekly. The 1501 card portal (accessible through the UTSA website) shows your remaining swipes, Dining Dollars balance, and Rowdy Bucks balance. If you're on a block plan, track your weekly swipe usage so you don't run out mid-semester.

Dining Dollars expire at semester end; Rowdy Bucks don't. When you add money to your 1501 card, choose Rowdy Bucks for any amount you might not spend before finals.

If you have swipes left at the end of each week that you're not going to use, they disappear — bring a friend on a guest swipe or just go for a snack. Wasted swipes are money you already paid.

---

COMMON FIRST-YEAR MISTAKES

Eating the same thing every day: The menu at Roadrunner Café rotates and there's more variety than it first appears. Give yourself the first two weeks to try different stations before you lock into a routine.

Only eating during peak hours: If all your classes end at noon and you automatically go to lunch with everyone else, you will wait in line every day. A small schedule adjustment pays off for the whole semester.

Not knowing that meal swipes only work at Roadrunner Café: The Sombrilla, Library Café, and food trucks do not accept meal swipes. This surprises a lot of freshmen the first time they try to swipe at Chick-fil-A.

Forgetting to eat breakfast: Roadrunner Café breakfast is the best meal of the day and the most underused. The dining hall is calm, the made-to-order omelets and waffles are fresh, and you're getting a real meal before class. Worth waking up for.

---

WHEN THE DINING HALL GETS BORING

It will. Usually around week 4-6. Strategies that help:

1. Mix up locations — the Sombrilla, Rowdy Cart, and food trucks offer genuine variety
2. Cook or bring food from home — even a few meals per week from your dorm room or apartment changes the monotony
3. Check what day has the best dinner — Monday through Wednesday tends to have the best variety at Roadrunner Café
4. Try the daily special — the menu board shows it; sometimes it's something worth breaking your routine for

GETTING HELP

For dietary restrictions or allergen concerns: dining@utsa.edu — they're responsive and helpful.
For meal plan questions: The 1501 card office in the Student Union building handles balance issues, refunds, and plan changes.
For general campus food tips: r/UTSA and the UTSA Discord #campus-food channel have active communities that answer questions.
