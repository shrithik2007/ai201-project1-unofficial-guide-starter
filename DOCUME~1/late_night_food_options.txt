ON-CAMPUS AFTER 9PM: LIMITED

The hard reality of UTSA's main campus: after 9pm on weekdays (8pm on Fridays, 8pm on weekends), there is no hot food available on campus. The dining options close and don't reopen until morning. What remains:

Vending machines: Every academic building has them. Prices are typical vending machine prices ($1.50–$3.50). Options are chips, candy bars, crackers, granola bars, and sometimes microwaveable items if your building has a microwave nearby. Not a meal, but it's something.

Residence Hall vending: If you live in the dorms, your building likely has additional vending options. Some residence halls also have small communal microwaves in common areas where you can heat up your own food.

The Campus Market (in some residence halls): Some UTSA residence halls have small convenience stores called the Campus Market. Hours vary by location — some are open until midnight. They carry snacks, beverages, instant noodles, frozen burritos, and basic grocery staples. Check if your specific residence hall has one and note its hours.

NEAR-CAMPUS OPTIONS (requiring transportation)

McDonald's on Fredericksburg Road: About 7 minutes by car, open 24 hours. The closest 24-hour food option to UTSA's main campus. Consistently mentioned as the go-to late-night option.

Taco Cabana on Wurzbach Parkway: About 8 minutes, open 24 hours. Better quality late-night Tex-Mex than McDonald's. Student favorite for post-midnight food runs during finals.

H-E-B on Babcock Road: About 10 minutes, open until midnight (check current hours). Good for stocking your room with snacks, instant meals, and real grocery items. Better value than any campus option for bulk snack buying.

Whataburger on Loop 1604: 24 hours, about 12 minutes. Whataburger at 2am during finals is a UTSA tradition at this point based on how often it gets mentioned in this subreddit.

Denny's on San Pedro Avenue: About 15 minutes, open 24 hours. Good for a full sit-down late-night meal when you need something that feels like real food.

DELIVERY OPTIONS (no car needed)

DoorDash and Uber Eats both deliver to UTSA's main campus. Service is generally available until midnight or later depending on the restaurant. Tips for campus delivery:

Use "Meet at entrance" for residence halls — drivers often can't navigate the internal campus roads easily, especially at night. Meeting at a campus entrance or parking lot entrance speeds things up.

The UTSA campus address for most delivery apps is the main entrance on UTSA Circle. Add specific building or residence hall instructions in the delivery notes.

Doordash has a student discount program — check if your .edu email is eligible for DashPass at a reduced rate. During finals several students have shared promo codes in r/UTSA.

Popular late-night delivery orders from r/UTSA: Whataburger (anything), Taco Cabana (bean and cheese tacos), McDonald's McFlurry runs, pizza from local shops.

FINALS WEEK SPECIFICALLY

During finals week, some campus organizations set up late-night study snack events. Check the UTSA Events calendar and your college's social media in the week before finals — it's common for student organizations and sometimes departments to host free food events for studying students. These are underadvertised and worth knowing about.

The UTSA library (John Peace Library) is open 24 hours during finals week. The Library Café itself closes at its normal time, but the building being open means you can bring your own food in.
