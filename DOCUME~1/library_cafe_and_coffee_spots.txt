SOURCE: UTSA Discord server #study-spots channel + Reddit r/UTSA
DATE: 2023-2024 academic year

Thread: "Ranking every coffee/café option on campus for studying"

---

LIBRARY CAFÉ (John Peace Library, Ground Floor)

The Library Café is the best sit-and-study café on campus by a wide margin. It's open until 10pm on weekdays which is much later than any other food option. The embedded Starbucks is full-service — lattes, pour-overs, cold brew, the full menu. The baristas are competent and consistent.

During regular semester weeks the Library Café is manageable — maybe a 5-7 minute wait for espresso drinks. During midterms and finals, the wait balloons to 15-20 minutes. The mobile order option on the Starbucks app works here and is the right move during busy periods. Order on your phone while you're still walking over, pick it up from the mobile counter, skip the line entirely.

The grab-and-go section at the Library Café entrance has pre-made sandwiches, wraps, and salads that are restocked in the morning. By afternoon some of the fresher options are gone but the basics are usually available. If you need food fast between classes and you're already in the library, this section is underused and has no wait.

"I do all my serious studying in the library and the café is what makes it work for me. The $6 iced latte is expensive by broke-student standards but I get 3-4 hours of studying done so the value-per-hour math works out." — library_regular_utsa

"The Library Café closes at 6pm on Fridays which is annoying. Friday evening study sessions get cut off. Know this in advance." — friday_studier_2023

---

JPL STARBUCKS (Jefferson Hall)

The JPL Starbucks is less crowded than the Library Café location most of the time, especially in the mornings. It closes earlier (7pm on weekdays, 3pm Saturday) which limits its utility for late-night studying. The quality is identical — it's the same licensed Starbucks operation.

"My preference is JPL Starbucks in the morning before 10am — short lines, fast service, and the building is quieter. After 2pm I switch to the Library Café because JPL closes too early." — morning_coffee_routine

"For people who don't already know: both campus Starbucks locations are licensed stores, not company-owned. That means they accept Starbucks app payment and Dining Dollars. The rewards points accumulate on your app. It's a small thing but it adds up." — starbucks_rewards_utsa

---

ROADRUNNER CAFÉ COFFEE

The coffee at Roadrunner Café is basic drip coffee. It's included with your meal swipe. It's fine as a morning caffeine hit if you're already eating breakfast there. Don't expect anything more than standard institutional drip. There's no espresso, no cold brew, no specialty drinks. Students who need a real coffee drink go to one of the Starbucks locations.

---

OFF-CAMPUS OPTIONS WORTH KNOWING

Local Brew (about 8 minutes from campus): Independent coffee shop with better espresso than Starbucks and a more focused study environment. No Dining Dollars accepted. Cash or card. Popular with graduate students on the weekends.

H-E-B bakery café (Babcock Rd): If you're already making an H-E-B run, their in-store café has decent coffee and freshly baked pastries at substantially lower prices than campus. Not worth a trip just for coffee but good to know.

"Once I discovered that I could get a good cortado at Local Brew for the same price as a Starbucks latte, I stopped going to campus coffee as much. The problem is transportation — you need a car or a friend with one." — coffee_snob_utsa_grad

---

BEST TIMES TO GET COFFEE ON CAMPUS

Library Café: 7:30-9am (shortest lines), 2-4pm (after lunch rush), avoid 11am-1pm and finals week any time
JPL Starbucks: Mornings before 10am (best option if heading to class in that building)
Roadrunner Café coffee: Any time during breakfast hours if you're already eating there — it's free with your meal swipe and the wait is zero
