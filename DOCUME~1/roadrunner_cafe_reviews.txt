SOURCE: Reddit r/UTSA — thread "Honest review of Roadrunner Café after a full semester"
DATE: Fall 2023

Roadrunner Café is the main all-you-can-eat dining hall on the UTSA main campus. Here are honest student takes from a full semester of eating there.

---

Review 1 (username: rowdy_runner_22):
The breakfast at Roadrunner Café is genuinely the best meal of the day. The made-to-order omelets are fresh and the staff are super friendly in the morning. Avoid going between 11:45am and 1:15pm — the lunch rush is brutal and the lines for the grill station can be 20+ minutes. I usually hit it at 11am or after 1:30pm and never wait more than 5 minutes. The waffle station disappears around 10:30am so don't sleep in if that's your thing.

---

Review 2 (username: cs_sophomore_utsa):
Honestly the food quality has gone downhill since the start of the semester. The pasta bar used to have real variety but now it's the same three sauces on rotation. The salad bar is always well-stocked though and they refresh it frequently. One thing that's genuinely good: the stir-fry station. Tell them exactly what you want and they make it fresh right there. Takes about 4 minutes but it's worth it compared to the steam table stuff that's been sitting for an hour.

---

Review 3 (username: freshman_2024_utsa):
This is my first time living on campus and Roadrunner Café is fine but nothing special. Meal swipes are $14 face value which feels expensive when the entrees are hit or miss. The pizza is mediocre — thin, greasy, and they only seem to restock it every 30-45 minutes during peak hours so by the time you get there it's been sitting out. The dessert section actually slaps though. The soft-serve machine works 90% of the time and the cookies are baked fresh in the afternoon, usually around 3pm.

---

Review 4 (username: upperclassman_transfer):
Transferred from a school with a much better dining hall so Roadrunner Café was a disappointment. That said, there are strategies. The made-to-order stations (stir-fry, grill, omelets) are consistently better than anything on the steam tables. The daily specials board sometimes has genuinely good stuff — check it on your way in. The dining hall closes at 9pm Sunday through Thursday and 8pm on Fridays, which is annoying for anyone who studies late. They don't do a last-call seating so if you get there at 8:55pm on a weekday they'll let you in but they're already pulling food.

---

Review 5 (username: pre_med_utsa_23):
The vegetarian and vegan options at Roadrunner Café are decent but inconsistently labeled. Sometimes a dish will be marked vegan and have cheese on it. I've learned to ask the staff directly. The vegan station near the back is usually reliable — they keep that one dedicated and cross-contamination is less of an issue. If you have any serious dietary restrictions, email Dining Services before the semester — they'll set you up with a guide and you can get accommodations.

---

Review 6 (username: commuter_no_meal_plan):
I don't have a meal plan so I pay cash/Rowdy Bucks at Roadrunner Café. Cash price for a meal is $14.50 for students which is genuinely not worth it compared to off-campus options. If you're a commuter, the Subway on campus or the food trucks on the east side of campus are way better value. The dining hall makes more sense if you have a meal plan where the per-swipe cost is baked into your housing contract.

---

Review 7 (username: resident_advisor_utsa):
As an RA I eat at Roadrunner Café a lot. The consistency varies a lot by day of week. Monday through Wednesday tends to be freshest and most fully stocked. By Thursday and especially Friday the variety shrinks and some stations close early. Weekends are a completely different experience — brunch only until 2pm, then a limited dinner menu. The weekend brunch is actually pretty good but dinner on Saturday night is grim. Not much open, food has clearly been sitting.

---

Review 8 (username: athlete_utsa_track):
The protein options at Roadrunner Café are solid for athletes. There's almost always a grilled chicken option, the salad bar has eggs and legumes, and you can ask for extra portions at the grill station without being charged — they're pretty flexible with athletes especially if you tell them you're on a training diet. The smoothie bar near the entrance is overpriced though — $6 for a small smoothie when the ingredients are right there. Skip it and make your own with the fruit on the salad bar.

---

Review 9 (username: grad_student_utsa):
The grad student discount is real — show your grad ID and you get a small price reduction. The hours are the biggest issue for grad students though. If your seminars run until 8pm you're basically out of luck for a hot meal unless you hustle. They should extend weeknight hours to 10pm for a campus this size. The coffee station is weak — watery drip coffee, no espresso. Everyone I know goes to the Starbucks in the JPL building instead.

---

Review 10 (username: sophomore_biology):
One thing nobody tells you: Roadrunner Café gets a deep clean every Sunday morning so it's genuinely the cleanest it'll be all week Sunday afternoon. The food is also freshest then because they restock everything. The noise level during peak lunch hours is overwhelming — I wear headphones. If you want to eat and study, go at an off-peak time or use the smaller seating area near the back which is quieter.
