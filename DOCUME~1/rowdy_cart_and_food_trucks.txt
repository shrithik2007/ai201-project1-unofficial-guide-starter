THE ROWDY CART

The Rowdy Cart is UTSA Dining's mobile food cart and, according to most students, the best food value on the entire campus. It's operated by campus dining staff and accepts Dining Dollars, Rowdy Bucks, and credit/debit.

Location: The cart rotates between several campus spots. The most common locations are near the Convocation Center, outside the Science building (SCI), and near the east entrance of the Rec Center. It does not have a fixed schedule posted on the UTSA Dining website, but the dining department posts daily location updates on Instagram (@UTSADining). If you can't find it, a quick check of that account will tell you where it is.

Hours: Typically 10:30am–2:30pm, Monday through Friday. The cart does not operate on rainy days or during campus events that take over its usual spots. It also does not operate during finals week reduced operations periods.

Menu: The Rowdy Cart specializes in Tex-Mex style street food. Regular menu items include street tacos (al pastor, carne asada, chicken, bean and cheese), tortas, elotes, and agua frescas. Specials rotate — the cart occasionally has birria tacos, breakfast tacos in the morning before 11am, and seasonal items.

Pricing: Street tacos are $3 each. A torta is around $7. Elotes (Mexican street corn) are $4. A full lunch runs $8-12. This is significantly cheaper than the Sombrilla food court for comparable or better quality food.

Student consensus: Almost universally positive. Specific praise for the al pastor tacos, the freshness of the food compared to dining hall steam tables, and the value. The main complaints are that it's not always easy to find and that it closes at 2:30pm which means no options for afternoon or evening snacks.

"The Rowdy Cart is the reason I didn't lose my mind eating campus food. The tacos are legitimately good. I start my Tuesday and Thursday with checking their Instagram to find out where it is and I plan my whole afternoon around eating there." — devoted_rowdy_cart_customer

"The al pastor at the Rowdy Cart is better than some actual taquerias I've been to. $3 a taco. On campus. This should not be legal." — taco_enthusiast_2023

"One downside: the Rowdy Cart gets a line around 12pm-1pm just like everywhere else. Get there at 11:30am if you want to beat it. The line moves faster than Chick-fil-A though." — efficiency_notes_utsa

INDEPENDENT FOOD TRUCKS (Near Engineering Building Parking Lot)

These are NOT affiliated with UTSA Dining. They are independent food truck operators who park in or near the Engineering building parking area on a rotating schedule. Payment is cash or card directly to the truck — no Dining Dollars, no Rowdy Bucks, no meal plan.

Typical schedule: Tuesdays and Thursdays, approximately 11am–2pm. The lineup changes but there are consistent trucks that appear regularly.

Regularly appearing trucks (as of Spring 2024):
- Taco Loco: Tacos, burritos, nachos — cash or card, $3-4 per item
- Korean BBQ on Wheels: Bibimbap, bulgogi plates, japchae — card only, $9-12 per plate
- Curry Up Now (occasional): Indian-inspired street food — card only, $8-11

"The Korean BBQ truck on Thursdays is my favorite meal of the week. Bibimbap with extra kimchi for $11. I eat one at noon and I'm not hungry again until 7pm." — korean_bbq_fan_utsa

"Taco Loco is consistent and cheap. Not as good as the Rowdy Cart but they're there on Tuesdays when the Rowdy Cart might be somewhere inconvenient." — taco_comparison_expert

"The food trucks near Engineering are not on any UTSA app or map. You have to know they're there. This is one of those things upperclassmen know and freshmen don't." — knowledge_transfer_goal

Staying informed: There's no official campus system for tracking these trucks. The best sources are: the UTSA Events Facebook group, asking in r/UTSA, and word of mouth from engineering students who see them arriving. Some of the trucks have their own Instagram accounts.

COMPARISON: ROWDY CART VS. SOMBRILLA VS. FOOD TRUCKS

For value: Food trucks (independent) ≈ Rowdy Cart > Sombrilla
For convenience: Sombrilla > Rowdy Cart > Food trucks
For quality: Rowdy Cart ≈ Food trucks > Sombrilla (with Chick-fil-A exception)
For meal plan integration: Sombrilla (Dining Dollars) > Rowdy Cart (Dining Dollars) > Food trucks (no plan integration)
