Chick-fil-A (Sombrilla):
The Chick-fil-A here is the same quality as any other Chick-fil-A which means it's reliably good. The problem is everyone knows that, so the line at lunch is genuinely painful. I've stood in line for 27 minutes before. My strategy: get there at 11am sharp or wait until after 1:30pm. The staff move fast once you're at the register. One thing: the mobile order app does NOT work here. Campus Chick-fil-A is not on the app. You have to order in person. — roadrunner_fan_2022

The Chick-fil-A in the Sombrilla is consistently the most crowded spot for lunch on campus. The food is good but unremarkable compared to a standalone location. The waffle fries are sometimes underdone compared to what I get off campus. Service is quick once you're at the front. If you're in a hurry during lunch, skip it. — business_major_utsa

---

Panda Express (Sombrilla):
The Panda Express here runs out of orange chicken by 1pm on most weekdays, sometimes earlier on Mondays. Not an exaggeration — I've shown up at 12:45pm to find they're out and won't have more for 20 minutes. The Beijing Beef and Kung Pao Chicken are more reliably available throughout the day. The portion sizes are standard Panda. The rice is usually fine. This is not a destination meal but it's fast and filling. — stem_student_utsa

I've had inconsistent experiences at Panda here. Sometimes the food is hot and fresh; other times it's clearly been sitting. The best time to go is right when they restock which seems to happen around 11:15am and again around 2pm. The student working the counter will usually tell you if something is freshly made if you ask. — junior_cs_utsa

---

Which Wich (Sombrilla):
Which Wich is the hidden gem of the Sombrilla in my opinion. The lines are way shorter than Chick-fil-A or Panda and you get a custom sandwich made fresh. The process is a little confusing the first time — you grab a bag, check what you want with markers, hand it to the counter. Takes a few minutes but the result is better than the alternatives. Their turkey avocado on wheat is excellent. — architecture_student_utsa

Which Wich closes earlier than the other Sombrilla spots and is closed on weekends. If you want a good sandwich and it's past 6pm, you're out of luck. The prices are a little higher than Chick-fil-A but the quality is better in my opinion. — design_major

---

Einstein Bros Bagels (Sombrilla):
Einstein Bros is best before 10am when the bagels are baked fresh. After noon the selection gets picked over and some of the specialty bagels are gone. The plain and everything bagels are always available. The cream cheese spreads are good. Coffee here is decent for a quick stop but not as good as the Library Café Starbucks. If you want breakfast on the go before 9am this is your best option in the Sombrilla. — morning_person_utsa

The egg sandwiches at Einstein are solid and faster than waiting for the grill at Roadrunner Café. I get the classic egg and cheese on everything bagel about twice a week. Prices are reasonable — about $6-7 for a sandwich and coffee. — pre_law_utsa

---

Jamba Juice (Sombrilla):
Jamba Juice on campus is overpriced even by Jamba standards. A medium smoothie is $8-9. The smoothies are good quality and made fresh, but this is a treat, not a regular meal option. The boosts and supplements are extra on top. I mostly use Dining Dollars I need to burn before the semester ends. — occasional_jamba_customer

---

Overall Sombrilla experience:
The Sombrilla food court is convenient but expensive if you're paying cash. Works best with Dining Dollars since it's a set budget anyway. The variety is decent — you can usually find something you want. The seating area fills up completely around noon and finding a table is stressful. The outdoor patio next to the Sombrilla is underused and usually has seats even during peak hours. — advice_from_a_senior

One thing that surprised me: the Sombrilla gets much less crowded after 2pm. If you can eat a late lunch, you'll have faster service and more seating. The food is usually still fresh — they restock most stations in the afternoon. — flexible_schedule_student
